Privacy Policy

Kindred Grounds Privacy Policy

Kindred Grounds is a coffee shop based in England. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our coffee shop, contact us, use our website or interact with us in other ways.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name and contact details, information you provide when making an enquiry or placing an order, payment and transaction details, and information about your preferences or feedback. If you visit our website, we may also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and information about how you use the site. We do not intend to collect more information than is reasonably necessary.

How we use your information

We may use personal information to provide products and services, process orders and payments, respond to enquiries, manage bookings or requests, improve our services, maintain business and financial records, and meet legal obligations. We will send marketing communications only where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing at any time by following the instructions in the message or contacting us.

Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases, as appropriate: performing a contract with you, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, complying with legal obligations, pursuing our legitimate interests where these are not overridden by your rights, and your consent where consent is required.

Sharing information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business, such as payment processors, website or IT providers, and professional advisers. They may use information only as necessary to provide their services to us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers

If a service provider processes personal information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by data protection law to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it was collected.

Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to operate, understand site usage or improve your experience. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any cookie controls made available on our website.

Keeping information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, request transfer of information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise your rights, contact Kindred Grounds using the contact details provided on our website or other materials. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our website or at our coffee shop.

Contact us

For questions about this policy or how we handle personal information, contact Kindred Grounds using the contact details provided on our website or other materials.
