Meet the Makers Behind Kindred Grounds’ Freshly Baked Treats

A freshly baked treat can make a coffee break feel that little bit more special. At Kindred Grounds, the people behind the bakes bring care and attention to the small details that make each one enjoyable—from its texture and flavour to the moment it’s served.

Baking is a craft of balance. Ingredients need to work together, and timing matters: a little too much or too little can change the result. The makers’ skill lies in paying attention throughout the process and creating treats that are a welcome companion to a cup of coffee.

There’s no need for a complicated occasion to enjoy something freshly baked. A pastry or sweet treat can be part of a relaxed catch-up, a pause in a busy day, or a simple visit to the café. The best pairing is the one you feel like having—whether that means choosing something familiar or trying a different bake.

The people who make the treats are an important part of the experience at Kindred Grounds. Next time you visit, take a moment to see what’s available and ask the team about the bakes. A little conversation can make choosing your treat part of the pleasure.
